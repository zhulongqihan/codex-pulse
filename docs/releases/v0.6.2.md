# Codex Pulse 0.6.2

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english) · [Download / 下载](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/tag/v0.6.2) · [Demo / 演示](https://zhulongqihan.github.io/codex-pulse/)

## 中文

修复低消耗周额度长期无法给出趋势的问题。周额度现在按较长的连续观察判断可分辨变化，避免把短时间内相同的整数百分比直接当作趋势消失。预测仍只来自同账号、同窗口的官方额度观测；没有可测变化、观测中断或数据陈旧时不会编造剩余时长。

官方未说明整数百分比的舍入方式时，接近额度上限也会保留更保守的触顶时间范围。

卡片在无法预测时说明原因，显示已知重置时间和本机今日 Token 实数。接口未返回的短周期窗口不再仅留下一个无法解释的破折号。额度百分比不动不代表没有使用；本机 Token 也不能直接换算成账号额度。

这是兼容修复，保留现有数据、外观和设置。可使用签名更新；下载受网络影响时，也可完整解压便携包、从托盘退出程序，再替换程序文件并保留 `data`。不覆盖已发布的旧版本。

右上角伴随窗仍面向 Windows 商店版 Codex。预测是条件充分时的趋势区间，不保证可工作时长。欢迎在[问题区](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues)报告可复现问题，或到[讨论区](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions)分享体验。

## English

Fixes slow weekly quota usage repeatedly losing its estimate. Weekly observations now use a longer continuous span to detect changes, rather than treating identical rounded percentages over a short slice as a lost trend. Estimates still use official observations from the same account and quota window. Flat, interrupted or stale observations never create a fabricated remaining time.

Time bounds near exhaustion also retain conservative uncertainty when the service does not specify how integer percentages are rounded.

When no estimate is available, the card explains why and retains known reset times and actual local daily token totals. A short window omitted by the service no longer appears as an unexplained dash. An unchanged percentage does not mean no activity; local tokens cannot be converted into account quota.

This compatible fix preserves data, appearance and preferences. Use the signed updater, or extract the full portable archive, quit from the tray, replace program files and keep `data`. Previously published releases remain unchanged.

The corner companion supports the Microsoft Store edition of Codex for Windows. Estimates require sufficient observations and do not guarantee working time. Please report reproducible problems in [Issues](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues), or share experiences in [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions).
