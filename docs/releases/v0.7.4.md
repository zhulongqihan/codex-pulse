# Codex Pulse 0.7.4

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english) · [Download / 下载](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/tag/v0.7.4)

## 中文

这次更新让额度与用量读数更容易理解：

- 可展开查看时间进度的计算依据，明确它来自官方额度窗口和重置时刻，与安装时间无关。
- 说明当前突出显示哪个额度窗口，并可查看实际返回的其他窗口；未知、陈旧和过期状态继续保留。
- 剩余不足一天时使用每小时分配参考，不足一小时直接显示重置前余量，避免把短时间余量换算成夸张的日均数值。参考分配不代表新增额度。
- 本轮每日观测明确说明周期范围和重新积累的原因；没有观测不当作零，也不拼接不同周期的数据。
- 在统计日期、观测和重置相关位置说明所用时区，区分统计日期与本地显示的重置时刻。
- 消耗分布明确对应所选时段的本机 Token，不代表账号订阅额度占比。

保留现有清爽／刻度、数据、偏好和同账号重启后的观测连续性。普通查看与展开解释不会触发模型请求。

欢迎在 [Issues](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues) 报告问题，或在 [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions) 分享建议。

## English

This update makes quota and local usage readings easier to understand:

- Expand the time-progress explanation to see how the official quota window and reset time determine it, independently of when Pulse was installed.
- See why a particular quota window is highlighted and inspect the other windows actually returned by the service. Unknown, stale and expired states remain explicit.
- Use an hourly reference allocation with less than a day remaining, and show the remaining quota directly with less than an hour left, instead of inflating a short interval into a daily rate. Reference allocations are not additional quota.
- Explain which cycle the daily observations cover and why a new cycle starts collecting observations again. Missing observations are not zero and different cycles are not stitched together.
- Identify the time zones used for statistical dates, observations and reset times, distinguishing grouped dates from locally displayed timestamps.
- Label the consumption distribution as local tokens within the selected period, independently of subscription quota shares.

Existing Clear and Meter styles, local data, preferences and same-account observation continuity after restart are retained. Viewing these explanations does not send model requests.
