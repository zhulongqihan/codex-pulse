# Codex Pulse 0.9.2

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english) · [Download / 下载](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/tag/v0.9.2)

## 中文

- 从账号页面等入口成功读取额度后，右上角卡片与浮窗会清除旧的“刷新失败”状态。
- 多个入口共享额度请求时，只发布一次结果；较早请求的迟到失败不会覆盖较新的成功读数或清除其缓存。
- 本地快照读取恢复后，即使账号额度的更新时间没有变化，也会清除本地读取错误。较新的更新到达后，旧手动刷新错误不会重新盖上失败提示。

网络或服务确实失败时，程序继续保留最近读数，并明确其不作为当前建议。重试倒计时表示冷却剩余时间；请求限流时继续遵守较长冷却。缺少某个额度窗口会单独标为未知，不代表其他窗口也读取失败。

升级保留 `data` 中的本地统计、偏好和同账号观测。修复不增加账号请求或模型请求。服务是否实时可用仍取决于网络、登录状态和提供方响应。

欢迎在 [Issues](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues) 报告问题，或在 [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions) 分享建议。

## English

- Successful quota reads from entries such as the account page now clear outdated refresh failures in the corner card and desktop widget.
- Entries sharing a quota request publish its result once. A late failure from an older request cannot replace a newer successful reading or clear its cache.
- Local snapshot-read errors clear when reading recovers, even if the account-quota timestamp is unchanged. A manual-refresh error superseded by a newer update cannot restore an outdated failure indicator.

Actual network or service failures still retain the latest reading and identify it as unsuitable for current guidance. The retry countdown shows the remaining cooldown, including the longer delay after rate limiting. A missing quota window is marked unknown separately from other available windows.

Upgrades retain local statistics, preferences and same-account observations in `data`. These fixes add no account or model requests. Live service availability still depends on the network, login state and provider response.
