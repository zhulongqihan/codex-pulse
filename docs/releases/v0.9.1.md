# Codex Pulse 0.9.1

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english) · [Download / 下载](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/tag/v0.9.1)

## 中文

- 展开预告说明可查看已到期、数据过旧、暂时无法获取、暂无有效预告和状态未知的区别，并显示实际检查时间与已知有效期。未返回的消息不会被推断成已撤回，过期消息不会继续作为活跃预告提醒。
- 主窗口、桌面浮窗与右上角卡片保留刷新期间收到的已读更新，避免较早发出的快照再次显示未读；窗口重新显示后也会重读本地状态。
- 已读状态成功保存后，后续快照读取失败会保留已读结果，并与保存失败分别提示；其他窗口成功标记后也会清除旧失败提示。旧通知的迟到错误不会影响新预告。
- Codex 插件显示相同的预告状态说明，继续只读本机快照；预告的检查时间与账号额度更新时间分别解释。

升级保留 `data` 中的本地统计、偏好和同账号观测。消息状态说明按需展开，紧凑提示保留原有布局；跨窗口更新只重读本地快照，不额外查询账号或发起模型请求。

欢迎在 [Issues](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues) 报告问题，或在 [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions) 分享建议。

## English

- Expand notice details to distinguish expired, stale, temporarily unavailable, no-current-notice and unknown states, with actual check times and known validity periods. A missing response is not treated as withdrawal, and expired notices do not remain active reminders.
- The main window, desktop widget and corner card retain read-state updates received during refreshes, preventing an earlier snapshot from restoring an unread indicator. Showing a window again also rereads local state.
- After a read mark is saved successfully, a failed snapshot reread preserves that result and is reported separately from a failed save. A successful mark in another window also clears the earlier save error. A late error for an older notice cannot affect a newer notice.
- The Codex plugin shows the same notice-state explanations while continuing to read only local snapshots. Notice check times and account-quota update times remain separate.

Upgrades retain local statistics, preferences and same-account observations in `data`. Notice explanations expand on demand without changing the compact layout. Cross-window updates only reread local snapshots; they do not add account queries or model requests.

Report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues) or share ideas in [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions).
