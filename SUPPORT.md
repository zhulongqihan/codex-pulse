# 反馈与交流 / Feedback and support

## 中文

欢迎使用体验、问题和改进建议。先查看[最新版本](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/latest)及[使用说明](README.md)，也可以搜索已有反馈，给相同问题补充复现信息。

软件内可从 **设置 → 帮助与反馈** 进入。摘要先完整预览，由你主动复制；它不会自动发送问题，也不包含真实账号、额度、路径、会话或原始错误日志。下载及数据访问的疑问见[签名与隐私说明](docs/TRUST.md)。

- **遇到故障：**[提交问题](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)，附软件完整版本、Windows／Codex 版本、相关外观、复现步骤与预期／实际表现。右上角提示的问题，请补充设置中显示的提示状态，以及 Codex 是否在前台或最小化。
- **有明确的功能需求：**[提出建议](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)，说明要完成的事情、当前不便与希望的结果。
- **想聊体验、想法或用法：**前往[讨论区](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions)，无需先写完整方案。欢迎中文或英文，也欢迎文档、翻译、设计和代码贡献。

我们会结合复现结果和影响安排改进，并在处理的反馈中关联提交或发布版本；[版本记录](CHANGELOG.md)与[发布页](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases)可查看已交付变化。

截图请先遮住邮箱、账号、私人项目和路径。不要上传 `auth.json`、整个 `data`／`.codex` 目录或原始会话。安全漏洞和敏感信息请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 的说明处理。

## English

Experiences, bug reports, and suggestions are welcome. Check the [latest release](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases/latest) and [user guide](README.en.md), and search existing feedback before adding reproduction details to a matching report.

Open **Settings → Help & feedback** in the app. The summary is fully previewed before you explicitly copy it; it does not submit a report or include real accounts, quota, paths, conversations or raw errors. See [downloads, data access and signatures](docs/TRUST.md#english) for trust questions.

- **Something is broken:** [report a bug](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the complete app version, Windows/Codex versions, relevant appearance, steps, and expected/actual behavior. For the corner indicator, include the status shown in settings and whether Codex is foreground or minimized.
- **A concrete feature would help:** [suggest a feature](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) describing your task, the current friction, and the desired outcome.
- **An experience, idea, or usage question:** join [Discussions](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/discussions). A complete proposal is not required. Chinese and English, documentation, translations, design, and code contributions are welcome.

Improvements are assessed using reproduction evidence and user impact. Addressed feedback links to the fixing commit or released version; see the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) and [releases](https://github.com/zhulongqihan/codex-pulse/releases) for delivered changes.

Redact email addresses, accounts, private projects, and paths from screenshots. Never upload `auth.json`, an entire `data`/`.codex` directory, or raw conversations. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities and sensitive information.
